# UX psychology list (2026-10-30): 100 design ideas for PC and phone

Made with the skill `ux-psychology-design` (smart defaults, endowed progress, value first, IKEA effect, loss aversion, anchoring) plus goal gradient, Zeigarnik, Hick, Fitts, Von Restorff, peak-end, chunking, Doherty threshold. Ethics: only real numbers, no fake urgency, no shaming.
Checked against CLAUDE.md's rejected lists (empty states, «next» card, 3-question setup, setup progress bar, «start here» panel, sync icon, form memory, typing suggestions, overload warning, day-end estimate, focus mode, haptics, one-hand mode, Asr wird reminder, «where you stopped», per-prayer streaks, all-streaks board, badges on nav… left out) and no audio.
Tags: [ك] both, [ح] PC, [هـ] phone.

## A. Today: goal and progress
1. From 80 % of the day goal the wall card shows «بقي 35 د» big instead of the percent [goal gradient] [ك]
2. Week goal as 7 pieces (a filled piece for each day that reached the goal) instead of one percent bar [chunking] [ك]
3. After Isha, when today would break the golden-day run: a calm line «سلسلتك 12 يومًا · بقي 20 د لتحفظها» [loss aversion, real numbers] [ك]
4. When the goal is reached the wall keeps a small gold «بلغته 16:40», no celebration [peak-end] [ك]
5. «ابدأ 5 دقائق فقط» on long tasks (≥ 60 min): starts a 5-min timer on it [lower the start barrier] [ك]
6. A task you started the timer on and did not finish carries «بدأتها» and stays at the top of its period [Zeigarnik] [ك]
7. «يومك في سطور» lists what you did first, then what was missed [positive framing] [ك]
8. Timer: the default category = the one you use most in the current prayer period (one tap to change) [smart default] [ك]
9. A task typed during a prayer period gets «بعد <that prayer>» in the preview line, removable in one tap [smart default] [ك]
10. After Isha: «اختر أول مهمة لغدٍ» — one pick among today's undone tasks or a new one, shown first tomorrow [implementation intention] [ك]

## B. Tasks and forms
11. The task box placeholder rotates real examples of the smart input («غدًا بعد العصر 30د !») [discoverability] [ك]
12. New event: repeat, reminder, colour, type folded under «المزيد»; only title, date, time open [progressive disclosure] [ك]
13. New event time defaults to the first free half hour after now that day [smart default] [ك]
14. Default reminder per event type (lesson 15 min, appointment 60 min) [smart default] [ك]
15. Task «⋯»: your four most used commands on top, the rest under a line [Hick] [ك]
16. Delete moves away from done in task rows and is lighter; opposite actions never side by side [Fitts, error prevention] [ك]
17. The multi-select bar says the result before acting («ستُنقل 4 مهام إلى الغد») [clarity] [ك]
18. Phone: a floating «＋ مهمة» in the thumb zone that opens the box above the keyboard [thumb zone] [هـ]
19. Phone: quick chips above the keyboard while typing a task (غدًا · بعد العصر · 30د · !) [less typing] [هـ]
20. A ticked task stays struck for 2 s, then slides down; no jump [feedback, Doherty] [ك]

## C. Prayers and worship
21. Tap on a prayer chip = «في وقتها» at once (the most common choice); long press opens the full menu [smart default] [ك]
22. Once the five are logged on time, the prayer row folds to one line «الخمس في وقتها ✓» [less noise, reward] [ك]
23. Adhkar chip with a small ring of how much of morning/evening you read [goal gradient] [ك]
24. Nawafil sheet: the most used kind first and bigger [Hick, Fitts] [ك]
25. Fasting days: the chip of a coming fast day shows «غدًا الإثنين» in gold the day before, removable [salience] [ك]
26. Late-prayer reason list: the reasons you used most first [smart default] [ك]
27. Ramadan days strip on Today: the days you logged filled, the rest empty (not a percent) [chunking] [ك]
28. Prayer map: the current month first with this month's on-time count big, then the grid [serial position] [ك]
29. Prayer chip colours: one meaning only (green on time, amber late, red missed, grey not yet) checked app-wide [consistency] [ك]
30. Bedtime chip after Isha shows the hours of sleep you would get if you sleep now [concrete consequence] [ك]

## D. Quran
31. Khatma bar: from 3 pages before a juz' ends «بقي 3 صفحات على الجزء 16» [goal gradient] [ك]
32. When the wird is done, a quiet gold dot on the Quran nav icon for the rest of the day [reward, no count] [ك]
33. Phone full screen: the 40-s page timer as a thin line at the bottom of the page [visible progress] [هـ]
34. The Quran bar shows your most used tool outside «⋯» (learned per device) [Hick] [ك]
35. Hifz plan: today's portion as a card with the lines drawn as 15 small bars, done ones filled [chunking, endowed] [ك]
36. Khatma card: the next milestone (juz' / half / third) named instead of only a percent [goal gradient] [ك]
37. Memorisation counter: the + button large and in thumb reach, − small and away [Fitts] [هـ]
38. Test mode: revealed words fade in, mistakes you mark stay underlined for the next review [feedback] [ك]
39. Riwaya select: the riwaya you use shown first with its name big, others below [smart default] [ك]
40. Ayah of the day card: one «اقرأ صفحتها» button, everything else in «⋯» [one primary action] [ك]

## E. Calendar
41. Today's cell ringed in the accent; past days slightly dimmed, future days plain [orientation] [ك]
42. Option: day fill as a bottom bar in the cell instead of a full fill, so numbers stay calm [readability] [ك]
43. Phone: tapping a day opens its panel as a half-height sheet over the month, not below it [context kept] [هـ]
44. Week view: today's column a little wider with the now line [Von Restorff] [ك]
45. A new event's date preselected = the day you are looking at [smart default] [ك]
46. Month header: the month's goal days as «12 / 30» next to the title [anchoring] [ك]
47. Multi-day pieces and Hijri occasions in two clearly different shapes (bar vs dot) [clarity] [ك]
48. PC: hovering a day shows a small card (hours, prayers, events) before the click [preview] [ح]
49. The day panel opens on the section you used last on that device (tasks / Quran / sessions) [smart default] [ك]
50. Past days with nothing logged show «سجّل ما فعلت» only on hover/tap, not a big empty block [less noise] [ك]

## F. Summary
51. One sentence at the top: the biggest change against the period before («القرآن +2 س عن الأسبوع الماضي») [serial position, anchoring] [ك]
52. Bar chart: a dashed goal line, today's bar outlined, future days as empty dashed boxes [anchoring] [ك]
53. Every comparison names its reference in words, not only ▲▼ [clarity] [ك]
54. Cards ordered by what changed most this period, with a «ثابت الترتيب» switch [salience] [ك]
55. Phone: the Summary opens on the week view with the first three cards only, «المزيد» below [progressive disclosure] [هـ]
56. Records card: a new record this period is marked «جديد» in gold [Von Restorff] [ك]
57. Category colours in charts fixed per category everywhere (Summary, wall, calendar) [consistency] [ك]
58. Month view numbers rounded («6.5 س» not «6 س 31 د») with the exact value on tap [cognitive load] [ك]
59. Prayer card shows «28 من 35 في وقتها» before the percent [concrete numbers] [ك]
60. The Summary tools menu shows the three tools you used last at its top [Hick] [ك]

## G. Notes
61. New note: the title filled from the first line until you write one [smart default] [ك]
62. New note in a folder inherits the folder's tag and colour [smart default] [ك]
63. Phone: the note tool row sits above the keyboard while editing [thumb reach] [هـ]
64. Note cards: the first picture or the first heading as the card's visual, not only text [recognition over recall] [ك]
65. Long notes: a thin reading progress line at the top of the reading view [visible progress] [ك]
66. A note with ☑ items shows its checklist progress as a small ring on the card [goal gradient] [ك]
67. Folder cards show the date of the latest note inside [recency] [ك]
68. Empty search result: «أنشئ ملاحظة بهذا العنوان» as the one button [next step] [ك]
69. PC: the editor opens with the caret at the end of the text, not at the top [smart default] [ح]
70. Edit / read switch as two clear tabs, the current one filled [state visibility] [ك]

## H. Library and books
71. Course card: from 80 % «بقي 3 دروس» instead of the percent [goal gradient] [ك]
72. Reader bar: «بقي 12 صفحة على نهاية الفصل» when the book has an outline [goal gradient] [ك]
73. Course page: «الدرس التالي» as the one filled button at the top [one primary action] [ك]
74. A course you finished stays visible with a 🏁 and its finish date, not hidden by default [ownership] [ك]
75. Player: after a lesson ends, the next lesson card with a 5-s ring you can cancel (real, user-controlled) [momentum] [ك]
76. Book cards: the page you reached as a thin bar under the cover [visible progress] [ك]
77. Your own cover and rating shown first on cards you customised [IKEA effect] [ك]
78. Library home order: continue, then your folders, then «from the PC» (what you built first) [IKEA, serial position] [ك]
79. Phone: lesson rows large enough for the thumb with ⋯ at the far edge [Fitts] [هـ]
80. Book reader: the night/paper mode chosen per book remembered and offered first [smart default] [ك]

## I. First run and settings
81. First run: time zone and language prefilled from the device, country and calculation method guessed from them, each with «غيّر» [smart default] [ك]
82. First run: the real prayer times of his place shown as soon as the place is known, before the remaining steps [value first] [ك]
83. First run: «اختر لونك» with a live preview of Today [IKEA effect] [ك]
84. First run: «اختر فئتين أو ثلاثًا» from ready chips (study, work, Quran, sport) instead of typing [smart default, IKEA] [ك]
85. Settings search: when empty, shows the five settings people change most often [Hick] [ك]
86. Each settings group starts with one sentence on what it changes [clarity] [ك]
87. Destructive settings (restore, reset) in red text only inside «منطقة الحذر», never in the main list [error prevention] [ك]
88. Settings switches show their state in words on phones («مفعّل») next to the switch [state visibility] [هـ]
89. «أقسام ثبات»: each area shows what you have in it («المكتبة · 34 درسًا») before switching it off [loss aversion, real data] [ك]
90. Data health: «آخر نسخة احتياطية قبل 3 أيام» in amber after 7 days, green within 1 day [concrete status] [ك]

## J. Visual language, PC and phone
91. One filled primary button per window; the others outlined or plain [Von Restorff] [ك]
92. One spacing scale (4/8/12/16/24) applied to cards, rows and windows [aesthetic-usability] [ك]
93. Card titles in one style (size, weight, icon place) across all pages [consistency] [ك]
94. PC: wider Today reading width capped, so lines and cards never stretch past ~1400 px of content [readability] [ح]
95. PC: keyboard focus ring and hover state on every clickable card, not only buttons [affordance] [ح]
96. Phone: every bottom sheet has its main action at the bottom in thumb reach [Fitts] [هـ]
97. Phone: page titles shrink into the top bar on scroll so the content gets the height [space] [هـ]
98. Numbers that matter (hours, pages, percent) in tabular figures and one bold weight; their labels muted [hierarchy] [ك]
99. Loading of slow parts (library, books, Mawaqit) shows the layout first with soft placeholders [Doherty, perceived speed] [ك]
100. Toasts with an action (undo, open) stay 6 s, plain ones 3 s; the action button always on the same side [predictability] [ك]
